Privacy Policy

Auralis · Effective date: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Auralis mobile application (the "App") handles your information. The App is designed around a simple principle: your face data never leaves your device.

1. Summary

  • The App does not require an account. We do not collect your name, email address, or contact details.
  • All facial scanning, measurement, and scoring is performed entirely on your device. Camera frames, facial landmarks, measurements, scores, and scan photos ("selfies") are processed and stored locally on your device only.
  • We do not upload, transmit, sell, rent, or share your face data or photos with anyone.
  • Anonymous usage events (for example, which onboarding step you reached) may be sent to our analytics provider (section 5). They never contain face data, photos, measurements, scores, or questionnaire answers.
  • From version 1.0.3 the App measures which of our ads lead to installs and purchases using Meta's SDK (section 5A). Your advertising identifier is used for this only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. Face data is never part of it.

2. Information processed on your device

To provide its features, the App processes the following data locally on your device:

  • Camera input and facial geometry. With your permission, the App uses your front camera to detect facial landmarks and compute measurements (for example, facial proportions and symmetry). Processing happens in real time on the device; camera frames are not recorded or transmitted.
  • Scan results and photos. Your measurement results, category scores, and optional scan photos are saved to your device's local storage so the App can show progress over time.
  • Questionnaire answers. Your onboarding answers (such as goals and focus areas) are stored locally and used only to personalize your routine within the App.
  • Activity data. Your daily checklist, streaks, lesson progress, and locally generated usage events are stored on the device.

Because this data stays on your device, deleting the App — or using Profile → Delete my data inside the App — permanently removes it. We cannot recover it for you, because we never had it.

3. Face data

This section describes specifically how the App collects, uses, discloses, shares, stores, retains and deletes face data.

3.1 What face data the App collects

  • Facial landmarks. When you run a scan, the App uses the front camera to detect facial contour points — the outlines of the eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, and jawline — in the live camera frames.
  • Facial measurements. From those landmarks the App computes approximately twenty numeric measurements (for example facial thirds, canthal tilt, jaw angle, interpupillary-scaled distances) and converts them into seven category scores and one overall score.
  • Scan photos. A still photo of your face is captured during the guided scan so that the App can show you a before/after comparison of your own scans.

3.2 How face data is used

Face data is used for one purpose only: to compute and display your own cosmetic, informational facial measurements and scores to you inside the App, and to personalize the self-improvement routine the App suggests to you. Face data is not used to identify or authenticate you or anyone else, is not used to build a face template for recognition, is not matched against any database, and is not used for advertising, marketing, profiling, or any form of tracking.

3.3 Where face data is processed and stored

Face detection and all measurement calculations run entirely on your device, using machine-learning models bundled inside the App. No internet connection is used or required for a scan. Camera frames are analysed in memory and are never recorded. The detected landmark coordinates are discarded as soon as the measurements are computed and are never written to storage.

Only two things persist, and both are stored exclusively in the App's private, sandboxed storage area on your device: (a) the computed numeric measurements and scores, and (b) your scan photos, which are written to the App's own Documents folder. Nothing is written to your photo library, to iCloud, or to any server. We operate no server that receives face data, and no copy of your face data exists anywhere outside your device.

3.4 Sharing and disclosure of face data

We do not share face data with any third party. Face data is never uploaded, transmitted, sold, rented, licensed, or disclosed — not to us, not to any service provider, not to any analytics or advertising provider, and not to any other third party. No third-party SDK included in the App receives face data; in particular, neither our subscription-management provider (RevenueCat) nor the Meta SDK used for advertising measurement (section 5A) ever receives face data, photos, measurements, scores, or questionnaire answers. The only way face data leaves your device is if you deliberately choose to share a result image yourself through the system share sheet.

3.5 How long face data is retained

Face data is retained only on your device, and only for as long as it is useful to you. The App keeps a rolling history of your twelve most recent scans together with their scan photos; when a thirteenth scan is saved, the oldest scan and its photo are deleted automatically. Because no copy exists on any server, there is no retention period on our side — we retain nothing.

3.6 How to delete face data

You can erase all face data at any time using Profile → Delete my data inside the App, which permanently deletes both the stored measurements and every scan photo from your device. Deleting the App from your device also removes all of it. Once deleted, the data cannot be recovered by us, because we never received it.

4. Information we do not collect

  • We do not operate user accounts and do not collect identifiers such as your name, email, phone number, or precise location.
  • We do not sell personal information. The only advertising-related processing is the ad measurement described in section 5A, and its cross-app tracking happens only with your permission.
  • We do not create biometric templates for identification purposes. Facial measurements computed by the App are used solely to display cosmetic, informational scores to you on your device and are not used to identify you.

5. Anonymous usage analytics

To understand which parts of the App are used and where people get stuck, the App can record anonymous product events — for example "a scan finished", "the paywall was shown", or which onboarding step was reached. Each event carries a random identifier generated on your device, which is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID, or advertising identifier, and is regenerated whenever you delete your data in the App.

What is never included in these events: camera frames, photographs, your scan selfies, your facial measurements or scores, and your questionnaire answers. Those remain on your device, as described above.

When this feature is active, events are processed on our behalf by PostHog, a product-analytics provider, acting as our processor. Analytics is disabled by default and is controlled by a remote setting, so it may be off entirely at any given time. It is never used for advertising, profiling, or cross-app tracking.

5A. Advertising measurement (Meta)

We advertise the App on Facebook and Instagram. To learn which ads lead people to install the App and subscribe, version 1.0.3 and later include the Meta (Facebook) SDK, provided by Meta Platforms.

  • What is sent to Meta: that the App was installed and opened, that the subscription screen was shown, that a purchase was started, and — through our subscription provider RevenueCat — subscription purchases with their price and currency. These events carry an anonymous identifier generated by the Meta SDK.
  • Only with your permission: your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is included only if you tap "Allow" when iOS asks whether the App may track you. If you decline, Meta receives aggregated or anonymised measurements only (Apple's SKAdNetwork and Meta's Aggregated Event Measurement).
  • Never sent: camera frames, photos, facial landmarks, measurements, scores, questionnaire answers, or coach conversations.
  • Your choices: you can change the tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and manage Meta's use of this data in your Facebook or Instagram settings (Off-Meta activity). We can also switch this measurement off remotely.

Meta processes this information under its own Data Policy (facebook.com/privacy/policy).

6. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through your App Store account. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple's handling of your payment information is described in Apple's own privacy policy. If we use a purchase-management provider (such as RevenueCat) to validate subscriptions, that provider receives only anonymized transaction information (such as a random app-instance identifier and receipt data) — never your face data, photos, or questionnaire answers. From version 1.0.3, RevenueCat forwards subscription purchases to Meta for ad measurement as described in section 5A, together with Meta's anonymous identifier and, only if you allowed tracking, your advertising identifier.

7. Notifications

If you enable reminders, the App schedules local notifications on your device (for example, a morning routine nudge). No push-notification service is used and no token is sent to a server. You can disable reminders at any time in the App or in your device settings.

8. Sharing initiated by you

Some features let you actively share content — for example, sharing your FaceCard image or sending a challenge link that contains your scores. A challenge link opens a page on our website that displays those scores and then hands off to the App or the App Store; the scores travel inside the link itself and are not stored on a server. Sharing happens only when you choose it, through the apps you select (for example, your messaging app). Once you share content, its further distribution is governed by the receiving service.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and you must be at least 13 years old to use it. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Our advertising is shown only to adults (18+).

10. Data retention and deletion

All face data, scans, photos and answers live on your device only. The App keeps a rolling history of your recent scans (up to 12) and recent scan photos on-device. You can erase everything at any time via Profile → Delete my data, or by deleting the App. Retention and deletion of face data specifically are described in sections 3.5 and 3.6.

11. Security

Your data is protected by your device's built-in security (such as device encryption and passcode/Face ID), and by iOS application sandboxing, which prevents other apps from reading the App's stored scans and photos. Because no copy exists on our servers, the primary way to protect your data is to protect your device.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or the Turkish KVKK), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Face data, scans, photos and answers are stored only on your device and we hold no copy, so you can exercise these rights directly using the in-app deletion feature. Anonymous analytics and ad-measurement events are held by our providers (sections 5 and 5A); declining tracking in iOS Settings stops the advertising identifier from being shared. For any privacy question or request, contact us at the address below.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles data — for example, by introducing an online feature — we will update this policy and change the effective date above before the change takes effect.
