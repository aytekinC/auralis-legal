Privacy Policy

Auralis · Effective date: July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Auralis mobile application (the "App") handles your information. The App is designed around a simple principle: your face data never leaves your device.

1. Summary

  • The App does not require an account. We do not collect your name, email address, or contact details.
  • All facial scanning, measurement, and scoring is performed entirely on your device. Camera frames, facial landmarks, measurements, scores, and scan photos ("selfies") are processed and stored locally on your device only.
  • We do not upload, transmit, sell, rent, or share your face data or photos with anyone.
  • Usage statistics (for example, which onboarding step you reached) are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us or to third parties.

2. Information processed on your device

To provide its features, the App processes the following data locally on your device:

  • Camera input and facial geometry. With your permission, the App uses your front camera to detect facial landmarks and compute measurements (for example, facial proportions and symmetry). Processing happens in real time on the device; camera frames are not recorded or transmitted.
  • Scan results and photos. Your measurement results, category scores, and optional scan photos are saved to your device's local storage so the App can show progress over time.
  • Questionnaire answers. Your onboarding answers (such as goals and focus areas) are stored locally and used only to personalize your routine within the App.
  • Activity data. Your daily checklist, streaks, lesson progress, and locally generated usage events are stored on the device.

Because this data stays on your device, deleting the App — or using Profile → Delete my data inside the App — permanently removes it. We cannot recover it for you, because we never had it.

3. Information we do not collect

  • We do not operate user accounts and do not collect identifiers such as your name, email, phone number, or precise location.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not create biometric templates for identification purposes. Facial measurements computed by the App are used solely to display cosmetic, informational scores to you on your device and are not used to identify you.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through your App Store account. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple's handling of your payment information is described in Apple's own privacy policy. If we use a purchase-management provider (such as RevenueCat) to validate subscriptions, that provider receives only anonymized transaction information (such as a random app-instance identifier and receipt data) — never your face data, photos, or questionnaire answers.

5. Notifications

If you enable reminders, the App schedules local notifications on your device (for example, a morning routine nudge). No push-notification service is used and no token is sent to a server. You can disable reminders at any time in the App or in your device settings.

6. Sharing initiated by you

Some features let you actively share content — for example, sharing your FaceCard image or sending a challenge link that contains your scores. Sharing happens only when you choose it, through the apps you select (for example, your messaging app). Once you share content, its further distribution is governed by the receiving service.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and you must be at least 13 years old to use it. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children — as described above, the App does not collect personal information from any user.

8. Data retention and deletion

All App data lives on your device. The App keeps a rolling history of your recent scans (up to 12) and recent scan photos on-device. You can erase everything at any time via Profile → Delete my data, or by deleting the App.

9. Security

Your data is protected by your device's built-in security (such as device encryption and passcode/Face ID). Because no copy exists on our servers, the primary way to protect your data is to protect your device.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or the Turkish KVKK), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Because the App stores all data locally on your device and we hold no copy, you can exercise these rights directly using the in-app deletion feature. For any privacy question or request, contact us at the address below.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles data — for example, by introducing an online feature — we will update this policy and change the effective date above before the change takes effect.
